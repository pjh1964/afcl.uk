We Make Bespoke Sofas & Chairs

We've been crafting custom upholstery in Buckinghamshire for more than 50 years. In that time, we've helped thousands of clients to realise their dream sofas and chairs.

Bespoke upholstery is our forté. We enjoy producing exactly what our clients want, whether that's simply using a certain combination of fabrics and polishing legs to a specific colour.

On other occasions, dimensions might need to change and some special requirements may also need to be met. That's fine! We're an upholstery workshop that creates things from scratch, not an importer of ready-made wares. If you can imagine it, we can probably make it!

Our furniture is built to last, so take as much time as needed to make what is a long-term decision. We encourage clients to make an appointment to visit our showroom to discuss ideas. Whilst here, browse the fabric collections, enjoy a coffee, and even look around the workshop if that's of interest to you. We never pressurise customers to buy. If our product isn't right for your circumstances, we'll say so!

Most importantly, we want you to be delighted with your investment.

That's why many clients return years later for re-upholstery of existing loved pieces that have served them well, or to add to their collections.

Do come to see us at our showroom in High Wycombe. A warm welcome awaits you!

Soho - Our Featured Collection

A classic mid-century sectional, the Soho group can be configured in multiple ways to fit any space. Arms can be placed on the left or right of any unit, or left off altother.

If a special size is needed for your room, this is easily accommodated by the workshop which will hand-build the units in any widths to suit a project's needs.

The plinth is solid mahogany as standard, but other timbers can be used including maple, beech, ash, or oak - to name a few. We can even upholster the plinth - the choice is yours!
